# Cyberbane changelog

The major changes in each version, newest first. Your progress always carries over.

**Jump to:** [2.7](#27) · [2.6](#26) · [2.5](#25) · [2.4](#24) · [2.0 to 2.3](#20-to-23) · [1.x](#1x) · [0.1](#01)

## 2.7

### 2.7.6 (24 November 2026)
- **Crew wars.** Pick a gang and who goes, and see the odds before they leave. Win and you take their money, and their war chest and some of the district's hidden stashes go on your map.
- **Your crew takes districts.** Win enough and a district is yours, in your crew's colours, with a street tax every day.
- **Chippin' In.** In a fight with an apex squad, one of your crew may come and get you back on your feet.
- **One crew member at a time.** Call or send home just one of them, rename anyone, and read each one's texts in their own thread, with a group chat for the whole crew.
- **Fenix Rebirth support.** Getting revived counts as a death, so your killer becomes a Rival.
- **Fixed:** enemies appearing right behind you, Rivals frozen and stuck together, downed enemies vanishing before you could finish them, crew sharing a car seat, and crew wins not counting when you sent them home.
- **Easier Hub:** a clearer Crew page with fewer duplicate buttons.
- For translators: new sections `DefineCrewWar` and `DefineEpithets`. Rival nicknames can be translated.

### 2.7.5 (23 November 2026)
- **Much Better Contracts support.** Its contracts count as jobs, and nobody new is sent into a contract fight.
- **Fixed:** a car you took from a gang could be deleted with you in it.

### 2.7.4 (23 November 2026)
- **Fixed:** a crash with a translation installed.
- The Aldecaldos, the Wraiths and the corporations drive their own painted cars, and the Wraiths keep to the Badlands.
- Crew lists show more than ten, each crew member keeps their look, and you can talk to a downed Rival while crouched.

### 2.7.3 (23 November 2026)
- **Fixed:** gang meetings that never happened. Event times are now shown to the minute.
- Crews find their way to you in tight streets, and people on foot come in ahead of your car.
- A cleared stronghold leaves its stash behind.
- Loot from crews is on their bodies instead of going straight into your inventory.

### 2.7.2 (23 November 2026)
- **Fixed:** a Rival who couldn't be killed. Nothing Cyberbane sends is unkillable any more.
- **Gang homes.** Find each gang at its own bar or base.
- **The crew at home.** They guard your building, use your stash as their armoury, and you can talk to them.
- **Crew jobs, the kitty and challenges.** Send your crew on hits and bag runs, and answer gangs that challenge you.
- **Signature chrome and strongholds** for top Rivals.

### 2.7.1 (23 November 2026)
- **Home ground.** Nobody comes after you in your apartment building.
- **A moment of peace** after every fight and after a night's sleep.
- You can lose a crew before it reaches you.
- **Cyberbane can be translated.** Every line is in one file.

### 2.7.0 (23 November 2026)
- **Fixed:** a crash when loading saves, enemies that couldn't be killed, and Conduct dropping when you fight gangs.
- **Before you're known.** A quieter early game until your Street Cred reaches 20.
- Turf wars feel more like Night City: the Mox no longer end up running Watson.

## 2.6
- **2.6.8:** gang meetings happen at places you've found, Rivals stop chasing you across the city, and texts take a moment to arrive.
- **2.6.7:** gangs hold their fire while you talk to one of them, Smasher Encounters and Night City Remembers support, and a Rival's bodyguards keep out of "just you and me".
- **2.6.6:** fixed frozen Rivals, gangs bringing the police back on you and people stuck in scenery. Mod Settings is no longer required.
- **2.6.5:** gangs come for you by their grudge instead of a timetable, **How much you hear** for fewer messages, and street stories: bagmen, debt trips, ransom drops and sit-downs about something.
- **2.6.4:** Dogtown's Scrappers are a gang (Phantom Liberty), and your crew no longer leaves when you use Berserk.
- **2.6.3:** fixed crashes near Rivals and apex squads, a key to call your crew, a crew that walks with you, and words that stay on screen long enough to read.
- **2.6.2:** your crew can outgrow an apex squad, and names show in the scanner.
- **2.6.1:** enemies measured against your build, news that takes time to travel, picking a side, and gang hangouts.
- **2.6.0:** **your crew's kit**: weapons, chrome, training, sparring, work, wars, a crew name and a memorial.

## 2.5
- **2.5.7:** vendors, joytoys, fixers and ripperdocs keep their own conversations, and you can talk to Rivals mid-fight.
- **2.5.6:** a kill is yours only if you land the killing blow.
- **2.5.5:** clearer texts, choices, map pins and Hub pages.
- **2.5.1 to 2.5.4:** talk comes up for everyone it should, no scan needed, and you can talk to downed Rivals and people with their hands up.
- **2.5.0:** **a crew of your own**, with loyalty, betrayal and a weekly cut. The map remembers what happened, and the Hub is rebuilt as cards.

## 2.4
- **2.4.6:** the street tells your story: voices, the screamsheet, the morning brief, and answering back by text.
- **2.4.5:** run with a gang's crew, join a gang, ransoms, lies, informants and the sergeant's parley.
- **2.4.4:** Rivals with a story: facedowns, humiliation, comebacks from the dead, feuds, trophies and the Rivals tree.
- **2.4.3:** bounties and named hunters, corporations hiring killers, and gangs clashing in the street.
- **2.4.2:** trackers, open season, road ambushes and the Tyger Claws' assassin.
- **2.4.1:** lie low, lose them on the metro, and catch scouts and lookouts.
- **2.4.0:** war chests, backing a gang, setting gangs on each other, streets of your own and peace with terms.

## 2.0 to 2.3
- **2.3.4:** gangs at war settle things near you.
- **2.3.3:** gang gatherings and sit-downs, announced ahead.
- **2.3.2:** every gang has its own code and adapts to how you fight. Crews have sergeants, and anyone can surrender.
- **2.3.1:** the street reads you: witnesses, night and rain, district moods, your car and gun, wannabes and your lifepath.
- **2.3.0:** crash fixes.
- **2.2.0:** sit-downs are real fights, and gang life happens more often.
- **2.1.0:** talk to the street's own people.
- **2.0.0:** a living street: bagmen, favours, sit-downs, surrender and "nobody kills you but me".

## 1.x
- **1.9:** talk to people, with conversations, informants and Rivals' hiding places for sale. Rivals with a life: mercy, catchphrases, kin, calling out and bodyguards. Clothes and masks count, factions can be switched off, Rivals team up, and cyberpsycho breaks.
- **1.8:** smarter enemies, netrunners in crews, earned nicknames, and ambushing a Rival.
- **1.7:** hunters really search for you, apex squads fight as four, turf that lasts, titles that do something, and the Hub in the game menu.
- **1.6:** hunt your Rivals down, turf wars, and game version 2.31 support.
- **1.5:** warnings, scouts and cat and mouse, loot and bounties, and a hunt line on the HUD.
- **1.4:** messages in your phone, replying to gangs, weak spots, map markers, and masks that keep people calm.
- **1.3:** clothes hide your chrome, masks hide who you are, and every enemy gets a health bar.
- **1.2:** how you look right now matters, and arrivals come on a visible clock.
- **1.1:** apex squads fight like MaxTac, Rivals climb the ranks, gangs have leaders, and nobody comes into safe areas.
- **1.0:** the first full release. Enemies have nerve, and jobs build your reputation.

## 0.1
- **0.1.0 (25 October 2026):** the first public release on [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/35847): Renown and Dread, Rivals, hit squads, crowds that react, gangs that turn on you, titles and the Hub.
- **0.1.1 to 0.1.19:** early fixes. Witnesses have to see you, stealth takedowns count, and word spreads from where it happened.
