# Cyberbane changelog

What changed in each version, newest first. Your Cyberbane progress carries over from one version to
the next unless a version says otherwise.

## 1.9.2 (12 November 2026)

**New**
- **Rival strength** slider under Rivals. Rivals used to follow the Apex strength slider, which now
  sets apex squads only. Both default to 55. If you had changed Apex strength, set Rival strength to the
  same number to keep your Rivals as they were.

## 1.9.1 (12 November 2026)

**New**
- **Switch factions off.** A new Factions page in Mod Settings has a switch for every gang, corporation
  and the police. A faction you switch off is left completely alone: no hostility on sight, no crews,
  no Rivals sent or made, no tribute and no texts. Your standing with them is kept for when you switch
  them back on.
- The Gangs screen shows **Seen hostile** when a faction's people are hostile to you without Cyberbane
  being the cause (They Will Remember, another mod or the game itself).

**Changes**
- New Rivals from a fight now get their name half a minute to a minute after the fight ends, so nobody
  "has your name now" while still lying knocked out at your feet. Finish one off before then and they
  never come back.
- A killer who becomes a Rival sends you one text.

**Fixes**
- The police could turn on you on sight with no stars showing.
- Dying and reloading the same fight piled up copies of the same Rival and a stack of texts.
- Cards, the hunt line and health bars could stay stuck on screen after dying and reloading.
- With messages set to Phone, some messages still appeared as cards, especially right after a load.
- "Asking around about you" messages now follow the Whispers setting.
- The Gangs screen asks They Will Remember about hostile gangs whatever the compatibility setting.
- Better performance on crowded streets.

## 1.9.0 (11 November 2026)

**New**
- **Rivals team up.** A Rival sometimes brings one or two others from the same gang, more often if they
  run a crew. Each one fights, falls, runs or is spared on their own, and the rest carry on if the one
  leading goes down. Choose two or three per team, or turn it off, under Rivals.
- **Cyberpsychotic breaks.** A top-rank Rival who keeps losing to you may come back as a cyberpsycho with
  a new name. They never run, and they get no extra health or armour, so they can always be put down.
  Can be turned off under Rivals.

**Changes**
- Who becomes a Rival depends on who they were. Officers and elites are likelier to come back for you
  than lookouts, and a single fight makes one or two Rivals at most. Rival frequency now affects every
  way of making one.
- Killing a low-rank Rival usually ends it. Killing a high-rank one usually brings a successor.
- Killing an officer brings an avenger less often than killing a boss.
- **Sleeves matter.** Long-sleeved shirts, jackets and coats hide arm chrome. Vests, t-shirts, tank tops,
  dresses and sleeveless jackets leave it on show. Shorts and skirts show leg chrome, and jumpsuits
  cover arms and legs.
- Your clothes count the way people see them: your outfit preset, slots you have hidden, and clothes
  from outfit mods.
- The Hub says why a countdown is waiting (a job, a safe place, a scene), lists the Rivals out after you
  first, and shows which Rivals are lying low after their last fight.

**Fixes**
- V's head turned on its own in photo mode.
- Visible chrome on the Hub did not update after changing clothes.
- Fights near gig and quest markers count again: enemies there flee, knockouts can become Rivals and earn
  Conduct, and crowds react to you.
- Hunters lost track of you mid-fight near a job, and enemies who broke and ran could be stopped in place.
- With They Will Remember, a gang shooting you on sight could show as Neutral on the Hub.
- Strangers could be made into Rivals while your existing Rivals were lying low.
- When one Rival went down, others out at the same time could vanish.
- A Rival who fled their hideout could come back as a brand new Rival.
- Less stutter in big fights.
- Smaller fixes to crew netrunners and grenadiers, the Hub's Ambush button and the health bars at the
  top of the screen.

## 1.8.5 (11 November 2026)

**Changes**
- **Smarter enemies.** Shooters use cover and good firing spots and shoot on the move, snipers find a
  perch, blades block and parry, and one member of each crew throws grenades to flush you out. Apex
  squads and Rivals get a little more on top. With Much Better AI or New Game Plus installed, they
  handle this.
- **Netrunners join crews** of three or more and use the game's full range of hacks on its own
  cooldowns. They never use the hacks that kill outright or take you over. Netrunners from More
  Netrunners come along once you have met them.
- **Nicknames are earned.** A new Rival is just their name, and earns a nickname by ranking up or by
  killing you. No nickname repeats on a save while new ones are left.

**Fixes**
- With More Netrunners installed, a crew could be made entirely of netrunners.
- A cyberpsycho who came back for you was missing their extra fighting skill.

## 1.8.4 (11 November 2026)

**Fixes**
- The Hub said a gang's apex squad was ready when something was stopping it. It now says what is
  holding them back.
- Companions, including Jackie through Night City Allies, now fight the people Cyberbane sends.
- Hunters could follow you into the middle of a job. Every active job counts now, and anyone already out
  waits until you leave it.

## 1.8.3 (10 November 2026)

**New**
- **This save is another character** under Debug in Mod Settings, for two characters whose records got
  mixed up before this update.

**Fixes**
- One character's history (deaths, Rivals, a Start over) could leak into another character's saves. Each
  playthrough now keeps its own records, and saves already affected sort themselves out when the two
  characters have different lifepaths.
- Reset all Cyberbane data could keep resetting every time you started the game. It now acts only at the
  moment you turn it on.
- Choosing the Custom preset reset your sliders.
- The Hub's time estimates could be off for a whole session after skipping time.
- Starting over could mix your old Rivals up with your new ones.

## 1.8.2 (10 November 2026)

**New**
- **Ambush a Rival.** Pay on the Hub to find out where a Rival is hiding, then catch them off guard. The
  price rises with their rank. Needs Rival hideouts turned on.
- **Forget one Rival** from the Hub, with a confirmation you can take back.

**Fixes**
- A Rival knocked out and then run over could leave a marker stuck on the map and stop other Rivals
  coming.
- Loading after dying in a fight could hang on the loading screen.

## 1.8.1 (10 November 2026)

**Fixes**
- A crash when several enemies died at once.
- Extra cyberware slots, including those added by Cyberware-EX, now count towards your chrome.

## 1.8.0 (9 November 2026)

**Fixes**
- Knockouts count again: they make Rivals and count towards your reputation, Conduct and titles.
- Enemies who ran had too little time to get away, so they rarely came back as Rivals.
- Enemies who run and cannot get away now turn and fight.
- Someone you had just knocked out could be told to run.
- A wounded enemy still shooting at you from a distance counted as having escaped.
- Turning Morale off also stopped wounded enemies from becoming Rivals.
- A hunter could carry two map markers.
- A Dogtown save from 1.7.8 could show Dogtown held by another gang.

## 1.7.8 (9 November 2026)

**New**
- **Turf that lasts.** A district changes hands once a gang has clearly been on top of its holder for a
  few hours, and stays taken until somebody takes it back. Your kills count extra on a gang's own
  streets, and a gang that has rebuilt comes back for what it lost.
- **See who runs the place.** The line at the top of the screen names the district's holder or says it
  is contested, the game's map lists the new owners, and the Hub's Turf screen shows who is moving in.
- Gangs fighting on streets they have lost break and run sooner, and the new holders' lookouts, crews and
  vendors take over.
- **Turf patrols** (experimental, off by default): in a district that has changed hands, a few of the new
  owners walk the streets while you are around.
- **Titles do something.** The title you go by now has a real effect (better bounties, cheaper vendors,
  hunters losing you faster, enemies breaking sooner) and a few of the big ones have a cost. "Title
  effects" in Mod Settings turns them off.
- The Titles screen lists every title, earned or not, with what it takes, your progress and what it
  does.
- **Enemy of my enemy** slider: how much a gang warms to you when you kill its enemies.

**Fixes**
- Pacifica could never be taken. Dogtown stays Barghest's, and the Hub says why.
- An enemy who streamed out and back in could get their strength twice and a full heal.
- Too many enemies could run at once.
- One death could make two Rivals.
- Different kinds of arrival could land on top of each other.
- Gangs warmed to you while you were still killing them.
- Districts could change owners with nothing happening.

## 1.7.7 (9 November 2026)

**New**
- **Enemies who lose their nerve really run.** How likely depends on the moment: how hurt they are, how
  many friends have dropped, whether anyone is beside them, their rank and how much their gang fears
  you. Elites hold longer, bosses never run, and a gang with a vendetta is harder to scare. It happens
  less often overall.
- **Gangs lie in wait** (experimental, off by default): a vendetta crew may park up the road while you
  drive and pull out as you pass.
- **Passers-by react in stages.** They look over, flinch and step aside, freeze with their hands up, and
  only run if you are truly terrifying and close. Each person reacts a little differently.

**Fixes**
- Crews sat in their cars until you walked up to them. They get out when the car stops and come for you.
- Arriving cars drove far too fast and crashed into everything, and could drive straight past you.
- Several Rivals could share a name. There are many more names now, and none is ever reused on a save.
- A Rival's replacement could take the same nickname.
- An apex squad could come twice.
- Freeze reputation did not freeze everything.
- Enemies you killed a moment later could still become Rivals.
- If the last of a crew runs off, the crew now counts as beaten and the bounty is paid.

- Start over left an announced apex squad and the hunt line running.

## 1.6.4 (4 November 2026)

**Fixes**
- **The phone and saving work while people are still looking for you.** Hunters are only told where you
  are once one of them has actually found you, so you are not stuck "in combat" while they search.
- Searching is a real search now, and a Rival texts "found you" when they really have.
- A Rival waiting at their hideout knew you were coming.
- Knocking people out made you as frightening as killing them. It counts for a quarter now.
- A room full of knocked-out people counted as a massacre.
- Dread only ever went up. Your body count and the jobs you have pulled now fade while you lie low.
- Laying low did nothing. Three in-game days without putting anybody down now makes the city forget
  twice as fast.
- The Hub blamed chrome you had already covered up.

**How to bring Dread down**
- **Right away:** cover your chrome (sleeves, trousers, something across your face) and dress down.
- **Over time:** lie low. Your body count, your jobs and your heat with the law fade, twice as fast after
  three days without a kill.
- **To slow it down:** leave people alive, avoid finishers, blades, explosives and bystanders, or lower
  Dread gain under Reputation.

## 1.6.3 (4 November 2026)

**New**
- **Turf wars.** Hurting a gang weakens it, and a broken gang loses its districts to the gang at war with
  it next door. Let it rebuild and it takes them back. The street texts you when a district changes
  hands. A new Turf screen on the Hub shows who holds each district and who is fighting for it. Turn it
  off under Gangs. The idea comes from Gang Retaliation by NovaAntonsito.
- **Every crew is led by its apex squad**: four of the gang's best (a heavy, a shooter, a blade and a
  netrunner) with the crew behind them. The crew counts as beaten only when its apex squad is down too.
  "Crews bring the apex squad" under Gangs turns this off.
- Give a crew the slip and one of the people you lost can come back as a Rival.

**Fixes**
- Crews vanished in the middle of fights.
- Somebody flanking you could be removed from the fight.
- A Rival could be retired while still fighting you.
- Tips on where your Rivals were never came.
- Somebody you knocked out was counted as spared a few seconds later, mid-fight.
- A crew or apex squad that could not arrive on its first try was dropped instead of trying again.
- Paying a gang off did not cancel what they already had queued.
- An apex squad member could get the wrong role's kit.
- Too many of Cyberbane's people could be out at once.
- Knocked-out crew were never cleared up.

## 1.6.2 (3 November 2026)

**New**
- **Hunt your Rivals down.** A Rival who walks away from you alive gets seen somewhere, and sooner or
  later a rival gang or somebody on the street texts you where, with a pin on your map. Go there on foot
  and they are there, expecting nobody. Catch them before they react for extra renown. Leads go
  stale after a day. "Tips on where Rivals are" under Rivals turns this off.
- **Rivals in the scanner.** Scanning a Rival shows their name, such as Kenji "Who Ran" Morita, with their
  class underneath.
- Gangs that already want you dead now name their price once a session, so you can pay off an old
  vendetta from your phone.

**Fixes**
- Forgotten Rivals came back after loading an older save or quitting from the menu. Forgetting now sticks
  for every save of that playthrough.
- Start over could wipe the wrong save if you loaded a different playthrough straight after. A reset now
  only ever touches the playthrough it was chosen in.
- Sparing a Rival could make a second Rival of the same person.
- The first Rival on a save never texted first, never came by car, and could be duplicated when they fled.

## 1.6.0 (3 November 2026)

**New**
- **The Hub is part of the pause menu.** Press Escape and Cyberbane sits next to Mods and Mod Settings,
  with the game paused underneath. Everything on it can be clicked, and Q and E (LB and RB) switch
  screens.
- **Click a gang** to see who they are at war with, how many of theirs are down, what they are about to
  do and what it would cost to buy them off, with a button to pay. Hostile gangs are listed first.
- **Click a Rival** for their file: why they want you, their strengths and weak spots, and the mark you
  left on them.
- **Click a title** to go by it. Every title you have earned is listed, with the ones still to earn and
  what each takes.
- **Start over** button on the History screen, which asks twice. It clears everything you earned by
  playing and leaves your build, chrome and look alone.
- A **Mod Settings** button at the bottom of the page.

**Changes**
- **Cyberbane stays out of the story.** While you play as somebody else (a VR avatar, Johnny), while you
  are in cyberspace or a braindance, while a quest limits what V can do (fists only, a seat in somebody's
  car, a metro ride, a scene), or while a story character walks with you, nobody is sent, every
  countdown waits, and the street does not turn on you. Anybody already on their way is called back out
  of sight, with no texts and no cost to you. Companions you spawned yourself with a mod do not count.
- Kills made as somebody else no longer count as V's.
- **Allow spawns in safe areas** only opens safe areas now. It used to open up quests and scenes too.
- The Standing screen is shorter. Lifetime totals and your fighting style moved to History.
- Dread now says what the street does ("people are uneasy", "people are afraid", "people run").
- **Apex squad boss bar** is now **Rival and apex bars**, under Interface below **Hunt line on the HUD**.
  Your choice is kept.
- The Hub uses the game's own colours and is narrower.
- The Hub key and the Hub style setting are gone. Escape, then Cyberbane.

**Fixes**
- A Rival walked into a VR fight, and a crew attacked on the walk to the water tower with River.
- With five titles you could only switch to the last two.
- Turning off the bars or the hunt line during a hunt froze them on screen.
- The Standing screen could say people were uneasy while they were running from you.
- "Fighting mostly with Butcher" read like a gang. It now says "Your style is Butcher".
- Cyberbane's pause-menu entry was missing if another mod had removed the Mods entry.

## 1.5.5 (2 November 2026)

**New**
- **Where messages go: Off.** No phone messages, cards, notices, overheard lines or message tone. Gangs,
  apex squads and Rivals still come for you, loot and bounties still arrive, and the Hub still shows
  everything. The only thing you give up is being told.
- **Cars arrive on foot too** (experimental, off by default): gangs pull up and get out while you are on
  foot as well as when you are driving. If the game becomes unstable with it on, turn it off.
- **More ways to win a gang over.** Leaving one of their people alive costs you a quarter of what a kill
  does, and earns a little respect. Restraint alone gets you tolerated. Only hurting their enemies makes
  them like you. Sparing one of their Rivals counts for more.
- The Gangs screen says who each gang is at war with.
- Finishing the Heist now changes what gangs think of you.

**Changes**
- **Toasts** is now called **Show messages**.
- The Hub's lists are laid out in proper columns, the City screen is a row per district with a bar, and
  the Conduct bar fills from the middle (red towards cyberpsycho, green for clean).
- Your phone is spaced out. A message that arrives while the phone is busy goes quietly into its thread
  and marks the contact unread, and an identical message is announced once.

**Fixes**
- Enemies who broke and ran vanished while you watched. They now stay until you cannot see them.
- Text on the Gangs screen was cut off mid-word.
- A leftover file could stop Cyberbane, and every other redscript mod, from loading.

## 1.5.1 (2 November 2026)

**New**
- **When they come** setting. Timetable keeps the daily windows. Unpredictable has no windows: any minute
  could be the one. Reactive only sends a crew when you give a gang a reason.
- **Show countdowns** setting. Turn it off and the Hub and the HUD say "soon", "any moment" or "later".
- **Warnings you can prepare for.** The local broker texts that somebody is asking about you, or you
  overhear it on the street. The HUD line reads TRACKED in the last two minutes, names what is coming
  (SCOUT, CREW, HEAVY CREW, APEX SQUAD, RIVAL), and reads LOST THEM or THREAT ENDED afterwards.
- **Scouts.** A gang that does not know where you are may send one scout first. Deal with them or stay
  out of sight and the crew never comes. **Scouts before crews** turns this off.
- **Cat and mouse.** A hunter has to keep you in sight for a moment before you count as found (SPOTTED).
  Crouching, the dark and wearing the hunting gang's colours all help. Searchers sweep the area like
  guards, and getting into a safe area or out of the district makes them give up faster.
- Rivals are more likely to come for you on their own streets.
- Loot scales with your level, crews carry cash, and a rival gang pays a bounty for a wiped crew.
- Running from a crew or apex squad costs you a little with that gang, and they leave you alone for a
  while.

**Changes**
- Every countdown and warning varies in length.
- On Hard and Very Hard, crews are slightly smaller and further out, warnings last longer and hunters do
  not see or hear as far.
- With Immersive Shooting AI installed, apex squads keep only part of their damage bonus.

**Fixes**
- The HUD line could read THREAT ENDED just before a crew arrived.

## 1.5.0 (2 November 2026)

**New**
- **They tell you first.** A crew, an apex squad or a Rival texts before setting off, with roughly where
  they think you are, and there is a real gap before they arrive.
- **They have to find you.** Hunters arrive out of sight, head for where you were last seen and search

## 0.1.0 (25 October 2026)

First public release on [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/35847).

- **Renown and Dread**, built district by district and gang by gang, with a fighting style (Butcher,
  Ghost, Professional, Chromed, Netrunner, Brawler) and titles to earn. Both fade over time.
- **A conduct meter** from Professional (clean work, mercy) to Cyberpsycho (civilian kills, brutality).
- Kills, massacres, boss kills, Street Cred, police heat and visible chrome all feed your reputation.
- **The city talks.** People who recognise you say things as you pass, and big moments get a message.
- **Crowds react.** Pedestrians cower or flee as you get close, and once you are truly feared the panic
  spreads.
- **Gangs turn on you.** Push one to a vendetta and they warn you once, then attack on sight.
- **Enemies break.** Cut down enough of a squad and the survivors may run. Those who get away can come
  back as Rivals.
- **Rivals**: named enemies who rank up, carry traits, taunt you about your history, remember your deaths
  and come to find you. Kill one and an avenger may rise from the same crew.
- **Hit squads** from gangs you have pushed far enough. Give one the slip and a survivor can become a
  Rival.
- **Word spreads** from where it happened to the districts around it.
- **Money.** Vendor prices move with your reputation, feared gangs may pay you tribute (earn Feared
  Landlord), gig payouts grow with your fame, and you can pay a gang to drop a vendetta.
- **Disguises.** Masks and hoods make you harder to place, and a gang's colours make that gang slower to
  recognise you.
- Drivers panic and clear the street when you fight out in the open.
- **The Hub** (F7 by default): your standing, gangs, Rivals, titles and a log of what you have done.
- Subtle, Default and Dramatic presets in Mod Settings.
- A They Will Remember compatibility mode.
- Companions, vendors, quest characters and anyone in a scene are left alone.
