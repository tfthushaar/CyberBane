# Cyberbane changelog

What changed in each version, newest first. Your Cyberbane progress carries over from one version to
the next unless a version says otherwise.

## 2.6.2 (21 November 2026)

**New**
- **Your crew can outgrow an apex squad.** Crew members now climb on the same scale: every rank and every spar with
  you makes them tougher and harder-hitting, they pick up new moves as they rise (dodges, cover, shrugging off hits,
  their own Kerenzikov at Legend), and a Legend who has sparred with you is stronger than an apex squad member at the
  highest strength.
- **See where your crew stands.** Their card and the list show their rank out of six, and their file says exactly
  what it takes to reach the next one: how many more fights won, or a training, and how many more spars count as one.
- **Names in the scanner.** Scan one of Cyberbane's people and you get their name: a Rival, your crew (with their rank),
  the one you are sparring with, a crew member who turned on you, a bounty hunter, and now every member of an apex squad,
  led by the one their gang's warning named.

**Fixed**
- **A spar hits softly, as it says.** The softer hits in a spar were never applied, so a crew member hit as hard in a
  practice round as in a real fight. A spar also stays a round you can win as they climb the ranks.

Your progress carries over.

## 2.6.1 (21 November 2026)

**Fixed**
- **Calling your crew works in any fight.** The crew only counted Cyberbane's own hunters as trouble, so being jumped
  by the game's own gangers always said there was "nothing to call them for", and a crew already with you went home
  in the middle of the fight. While they're not already with you, their phone thread always has **Need you. Now.**,
  and they text back why when they can't come.
- **A Rival you put down no longer gives up their own hiding place.** Crouching over a downed Rival questioned them
  like one of their people. A Rival on the ground is now yours to judge in words: look at them and press F to spare
  them, finish them, or (one you have spared before) ask them to work for you. Nobody you grab, scan or question ever
  names themselves.
- **A gang's favour is on your map the moment they ask.** The lookout or the debtor was only marked once they were in
  the world, so "I marked it for you" could leave nothing on the map.

**Messages that feel like word getting round**
- **News takes time to travel.** It starts going round once the fight is over, takes longer the further away it
  happened, and never reaches you in the middle of a fight.
- **Fewer, better-spaced messages.** Hostile gangs put their price to you one at a time, a couple of in-game hours
  apart, instead of all at once after a load. Somebody coming for you is announced once: by your lookout, a friendly
  gang, or the gang itself, never all three. A gang's spoken warning is not repeated every minute, and a gang that
  knows what you have done to it no longer calls you "civilian".
- **Calmer defaults:** at least a minute and a half between pieces of news, a little more between overheard lines,
  and the line at the top of the screen shows only while somebody is after you. All three are still settings.
- **Gatherings say when:** "21:00 today", "03:00 tomorrow", and their pin keeps up as the day turns.

**New**
- **Enemies measured against you.** Apex squads, Rivals and named bounty hunters now read how strong you are from the
  game itself: your level and attributes, your health and armour, your best weapon and your chrome. Each lasts a few
  seconds of your own fire (longer at a higher strength setting) and hits hard enough to get through your armour, and
  they come ready for how you fight: ICE against a netrunner, blocks and dodges against blades, eyes that see through
  camo against stealth, their own dodges against a Sandevistan or Kerenzikov, and cover against a marksman.
- **Built like Adam Smasher, at your level.** On top of that, apex squads, Rivals and hunters scale the way the game
  scales its last boss: his own health and damage curves at their level, which follows yours, a share of his strength
  each (an apex member up to two fifths of him at the highest Apex strength, a Rival at the top of their climb up to
  half), and like him, no single hit takes more than a set part of their health, so an endgame build cannot delete
  them in one burst. Still beatable: the share and that limit are the settings' to turn down.
- **Pick a side.** A street fight between two gangs squares up first. Walk up to either side and tell them you're
  with them: they take you in, the others turn on you, and if your side takes the street they pay you and remember it.
- **Hangouts.** On a gang's own streets where none of theirs are about, a few of them now hang out nearby now and then,
  marked on your map: somebody to talk to, ask a favour of or join. Hard-to-find gangs like the Mox are the reason.
- **Distance matters.** A gang reaches you faster on its own turf and takes a while to come across the city. A Rival
  comes from their own district.
- **Cleared streets stay clear** (experimental, off by default, under Gangs). Wipe a gang out of a spot and for three
  in-game days their people keep away from it.

Your progress carries over.

## 2.6.0 (20 November 2026)

**Your crew, kitted out.** Everything below is on the Hub's Crew page. Pick somebody and press **Orders** for their
list; press **Name, colours, the fallen** for the crew itself. Orders happen as you pick them, and their texts come once
you are back in the game. A spar waits for the game, and clicking it again before then calls it off.

- **Give them a weapon** from your bag. Its mods and attachments go back in your bag first. From their next call they
  fight the way it is used (heavy, shooter or blade), on a body of their gang's that carries that kind of weapon, and
  it adds to their damage by its quality. Take it back whenever you like, and it comes back to you if they leave the
  crew. One who used to be a Rival keeps their own face; only the way they fight changes.
- **Have chrome put in.** Spare cyberware you own and do not wear goes in for good, up to four pieces each, and does
  for them what it does in the game, by the game's own numbers for that piece and its quality: a Mechatronic Core
  makes them hit drones, mechs and robots harder, subdermal armour adds its armour, a Sandevistan, a Kerenzikov or a
  second heart gives them one of their own. What the game lets only you use (critical hits, headshots, weak spots,
  attack speed) counts as the extra damage it would do on average. Chrome that only helps you (RAM, zoom, stamina) is
  not offered. Their text and their file say what it does, and you feel it the next time they fight beside you.
- **Ranks:** Rookie, Soldier, Veteran, Lieutenant, Enforcer, Legend. Their wins and their training together decide it,
  and every rank makes them tougher.
- **Send them to train.** They are away for some hours and it costs money, more each time, three times at most. They
  come back a rank higher, with better gear.
- **Spar with them.** Call one out for a timed round. They come at you tougher than usual but hitting softly, and the
  round stops the moment either of you is close to going down, or the time runs out. Nobody dies of it. Afterwards they
  are sharper for a day, and every three spars count as a training. Once a day each.
- **Put them to work.** An earner brings in money every day, more the higher their rank, and now and then comes back
  hurt.
- **Put one to recruiting.** One of your crew at a time can look for people worth having. The broker texts you a solo
  more often, even before the city knows your name, for a quarter less, and the solo arrives as a Soldier with a few
  fights behind them (a Veteran when your recruiter is a Lieutenant or higher) and more loyal than most.
- **Send them to start a war.** From Lieutenant up, send one against a gang that is not your friend. Won, the gang loses
  ground and blames you; lost, they come back hurt, or not at all. The weaker the gang, the better the odds. Your
  History keeps every war.
- **Name your crew** and **give it colours**, from eight. The name heads the Crew page; the colours go on your crew's
  cards and their texts.
- **Your crew has its own standing with each gang**, apart from yours. A war it starts sets that gang against it, more
  when it wins, and pleases the gang at war with them. A gang that lends you somebody warms to it; a gang whose people
  it scares into joining or kills cools, and so does one that kills one of yours. It eases a little every day. A gang
  that hates your crew is ready for its next war, so the odds drop. The Crew page lists where it stands, worst first.
- **Your crew on the Gangs page.** A card of its own among the gangs, in its colours. Pick a gang and the card says how
  that gang sees your crew; with nothing picked, it names the gang that thinks least of it. Click it for the crew's
  page. Each gang's words under the list say it too.
- **Remember the fallen.** Everyone of your crew who dies goes on a memorial: their rank, how and where they died and
  how long ago, how long they were with you, their wins, the weapon of yours they carried and the chrome you gave them.

**Fixed in this version**, from a read of the whole mod.

*Your crew*
- **Somebody who joins your crew stands down on the spot.** A ganger the game keeps hostile to you (the Scavengers,
  anywhere) went on sizing you up after saying yes, the detection meter rising, until your newest crew member opened
  fire. The same goes for anybody who backs down to you in words (see the street, below).
- **Your crew's name is saved as you press Save**, and the crew's screen shows it at once. It waited until you closed
  the menu.
- The sharper edge after a spar promised better critical hits, which never did anything: the game lets only you land
  them. It now counts as the extra damage it would do.
- The chrome you give your crew is now saved in a shape that cannot upset loading a save, for the living and on the
  memorial.
- Rank is the same everywhere: the card, their file, their nickname, their weekly cut and the text when they move up
  all count training as well as wins. A member trained to Veteran had a Veteran card but no nickname.
- A spar ends when the story takes over, a scene starts, or you walk into a job or a safe place. Loading a save in the
  middle of one no longer scores a round nobody fought.
- Answers to your crew orders on the Hub (no room for more chrome, the weapon is gone from your bag, training costs
  this much, the crew's new name) show even with Cyberbane's messages turned off.
- Switching the crew off in Mod Settings no longer gets you a "Not our kind of place" text.
- Somebody who joins by text after owing you their life costs your crew standing with their old gang, as they do when
  they join on the street.

*Rivals*
- A gang you have switched off, or one set to everybody's enemy, no longer makes Rivals of its own. Before, the roll
  could say yes and the Rival was then never made.
- A Rival's successor climbs to the rank they inherit, as the story says.
- A Rival who kills you gets the scar of the weapon used: a gun left no scar and a blade counted as a gunshot.
- A Rival you humiliate at the top of their gang's ladder can now break as intended. The rank they lose for it was
  taken before the check, so it never happened.
- A beaten Rival's comeback keeps to the Max rivals setting and the limit per gang; with no room, it is a warning.
- A Rival tied to another (lovers, a debt) no longer retires with them or calls the debt in while that other Rival is
  out in the world or after you.
- Loading a save clears a facedown or a taunt that was under way.

*The street*
- **Whoever backs down to you stops watching you.** Somebody you talk down, lean on until they fold, frighten into
  informing, buy a lead from, pay to call off a crew, or pay for protection: Cyberbane kept itself from turning them on
  you, and nothing told the game. A gang the game keeps hostile to you went on sizing you up, the detection meter
  rising, until they drew on the person they had just backed down to. Now they stand down the way the game stands
  people down, and stay that way while they are about, unless you hurt them. A ganger afraid of you stops sizing you up
  the moment you speak to them.
- **A toll you buy holds for that gang's people as you pass them**, the way they promise ("nobody of ours stops you"),
  for as long as it lasts. It held only against Cyberbane's own grudges.
- Paying off a gang, or settling a war, now leaves them neutral. Before, it made them friendly, so the gang you had
  just paid texted that you were welcome on their streets.
- A gang's first tribute reads as an offer; "as agreed" comes from the second payment on.
- A gang talking peace now simply asks you to send the money.
- A funeral after a Rival or a boss dies says whether you were the one who put them down, and the mourners answer you
  accordingly. A Rival somebody else killed still gets a funeral, and nobody there blames you.
- A scout who takes your bribe walks off for good, and their pin goes with them.
- Gangs talking about taking ground back say so only when it was theirs before; otherwise they have pushed the other
  gang out.
- When Cyberbane holds off because you are in a gang's restricted or dangerous zone, the Hub and its message say so.
  They called it a job.
- Lowering your weapon in a safe area follows the same rules on the way in as while you stay: never in a scene or with
  a conversation open, and turning the setting off inside puts the lock back.
- Gunfire no longer sends drivers into a panic near a job you are at, tracked or not.
- A Rival who steps into your fight because nobody kills you but them now holds the grudge and comes for you
  afterwards, even with Cyberbane's messages turned off.
- A corp you finished a contract for no longer opens a later contract on you with "We told you to stop".
- The bounty button on the Hub tells you when the money has gone from your pocket before it could be paid.
- With **Apex squads per day** at 0, no apex squad is announced either. The warning still came, for a squad that never
  did.
- In a safe place the doors stay shut to the people sent after you, and open for your crew, a friendly gang's people
  riding with you and the help you called. Your crew was left standing outside.
- Nothing new goes round the street while reputation is frozen.

*Words*
- The Nightmare title names the gang it was earned on.
- The Butcher title's requirement says "Kills in one district", which is what it counts.
- Lines with no district or gang to name say "Night City" or "a gang" in their place; a Rival made outside the eight
  districts no longer has "back in ." in their story.
- A masked V after dark is talked about as masked after dark. Seeing through a disguise names what they saw through:
  the mask, or their own colours on you.
- Your talk log lists phone threats and dares.
- A Rival's weak spot to explosives is told as explosives.
- A few lines no longer assume somebody is a man.
- The descriptions of Gatherings and Fights between gangs in Mod Settings match what happens.
- Every preset now sets how often gang life happens: choosing Turf war and then another preset kept Turf war's.

Your progress carries over.

## 2.5.7 (20 November 2026)

**Talking to people**
- **Vendors, joytoys, fixers and ripperdocs work as normal again.** Since 2.5.2, Cyberbane switched off the game's own
  "Talk" on anybody you looked at and gave it back by its own rule, which for people whose conversation the game sets
  up its own way meant never: joytoys could not be hired, and some people had no prompt at all. Cyberbane now leaves
  them alone completely.
- **Everybody else gets Cyberbane's conversation**, including people standing about with a "Talk" of their own, like
  two smokers by a fence or a Barghest soldier on watch: pressing F gave only the game's one-liner. Their own "Talk"
  always comes back afterwards.
- **F always does something.** When another prompt stayed in front of somebody (a door, a seat, another mod's spot),
  Cyberbane kept their "Talk" and their neighbours' switched off while it waited, so neither the game's prompt nor
  Cyberbane's came up. Now it waits a moment, then gives everything back and leaves that person to the game until you
  look at somebody else. A prompt the game is not actually showing no longer holds talk off.

- **Pressing Talk brings up the lines for people with a "Talk" of their own too.** For passers-by and anybody else the
  game gives its own "Talk", the lines went up and could not be seen: Cyberbane switched their own talk off again five
  times a second, and each time the game redrew that person's prompts and took the lines away. It is now switched off
  only when the game has really put it back. Their second talk prompt, the one the game shows once you have spoken to
  them, is switched off and given back the same way.

**Rivals**
- **A Rival at their own place gives you the facedown too.** Every Rival you tracked down at their place opened fire
  the moment they saw you, so the facedown never happened at all. Now, walk up openly and they step out to face you;
  sneak up or shoot first and it is still an ambush.
- **Talk in the middle of a fight to the people the fight is for.** A Rival down at your feet, somebody with their
  hands up, a Rival or a sergeant standing for a facedown or a parley: you can talk to them while the shooting goes on
  around you. Being in combat closed talk for everybody, so the mercy choice and the ransom could only be had once
  every last enemy was down.
- **A Rival who goes down mid-fight can be spared in words at once.** The choice to finish them, walk away or talk
  waited until the whole fight was over.
- **A Rival at the end of their rope may give up.** Badly hurt, a Rival who loses their nerve now sometimes drops where
  they stand instead of running, and it is your call: finish them, walk away, or talk to them. A badly hurt Rival could
  only fight on or run, and neither could be talked to.
- **A Rival walking up for a facedown waits while you deal with somebody else.** Busy in another fight, you lost the
  facedown: they turned hostile after about a minute without a word. They now wait up to four minutes for you.

**Fights**
- A ganger who has given up, lies at your feet for your mercy, or is talking to you is never turned back on you by their
  gang's grudge. Somebody who had just put their hands up could be sent at you again a second later.
- **A Rival somebody else kills is not your kill.** A Rival the NCPD or another gang killed counted as yours: on your
  tally and your titles, Renown paid, the street saying everybody knew who did it, and worst, the Rival bound to them
  texting "You killed..." and coming after you, or a successor sworn to avenge them on you. Now they are simply dead,
  their chair is filled, and nobody blames you. Your History says a Rival died, and not by your hand.
- Somebody you hurt and a car then ran down (a police car, another gang's) is not your kill.

**Bug reports**
- Cyberbane's log (in Cyber Engine Tweaks' `gamelog.log`) now says why a bagman, a gang event, a sit-down, a street
  clash or your crew did not come when their turn came, and what is taking up room for Cyberbane's people.

Your progress carries over.

## 2.5.6 (20 November 2026)

**Texts**
- **A text that asks nothing back no longer offers a Rival's taunts.** When a gang's leader dies, the two who want the
  chair text you an offer: kill the other one. Because both are Rivals, the thread offered "Come and find me", "Name
  the place" and "You'll lose again". Those offers, the winner's thanks, and a Rival texting that they are coming to
  help you ("Nobody kills you but me") now offer no answers. Killing the other one is the answer.
- A Rival who has named a place to meet offers no answers either. "Name the place" asked them for a second one, and
  "Come and find me" made them text "On my way" while they went on waiting where they said. Go to the place.

**Fights**
- **A kill is yours only if you land the killing blow.** A death counted as yours whenever you had hit them at any
  point, so a ganger you grazed and another gang then killed was put on your record: their gang's grudge, your
  fighting style, the Renown. Now whoever hits them last decides, and a takedown counts as your hit. Somebody you
  knocked down and somebody else then finished is no longer counted as a kill of yours.

**Messages**
- **A card no longer sticks on screen.** With messages set to cards, one now and then stayed up until the next message,
  minutes or half an hour later, instead of fading. Cyberbane now takes its card down itself two seconds after the
  game should have, if it is still showing and nothing else has replaced it.

**Quests and jobs**
- **Nobody breaks and runs at a job any more.** When you kill somebody, the people beside them can lose their nerve and
  run, and the one who breaks frightens everybody near them. At a gig that reached the job's own people too: a surgeon
  hidden in a box for his scene could come out of it before his time. Now nobody breaks or gives up at the job you are
  tracking, in a scene, while the story has the stage, or near any job's marker. They fight on instead.

**The Hub**
- **"Hub in the pause menu" works again.** Since 2.4.6 that switch in Mod Settings had come loose: it moved nothing, and
  if you saw it, it switched Voices instead. It is back where it was, under **Messages and HUD**. Off (the default), the
  Hub is the CYBERBANE tab at the top of the game menu, after Journal; with the game's radial menu, open Character and
  it is beside it. On, it is Cyberbane in the pause menu (Escape). Turn it on if you cannot find the tab. If you changed
  that switch since 2.4.6, check that **Voices for the moments** is the way you want it.

**Fights**
- **Your side's killing blows still count as yours.** With the killing blow deciding, somebody you hurt and your crew,
  a companion, a borrowed crew or a Rival fighting at your side then finished was counted as nobody's: a bagman you had
  shot was not robbed, a sit-down you and your crew cut down was not crashed. They count as yours again. Other gangs'
  killing blows still do not.
- Somebody you hurt and another gang then knocked out cold is not counted as you sparing them, as their death would not
  be your kill: no Conduct, no standing for sparing them, and no Rival who remembers you letting them live.

**Fixed**
- A favour to lean on somebody who owes money ended with "They paid up before you got to them" when somebody else had
  killed them. It now says somebody got to them first.
- An informant whose gang found out said they were coming "within the hour". The hunt comes a few hours later at the
  soonest, and the text says so.
- Switching off **Your crew** or **Run with a crew** while they are out with you sends them home. They went on following you
  until the fight was over or their hour was up.
- A Rival who was warned to be coming, then spared or switched off before they arrived, came straight in without a
  warning the next time they were sent.

Your progress carries over.

## 2.5.5 (19 November 2026)

Every line Cyberbane shows was read again for what a player could take from it, so that none can be read two ways,
none says one thing while the game does another, and none has a grammar slip.

**Favours**
- **A debt is a favour, not a hit.** The marker said "Owes the Valentinos", which reads as a reminder or a contract. It
  now says "Owes the Valentinos money", and its note says "A favour, not a hit: talk to them and scare them into paying
  (Intimidate or Antagonize). They should walk away scared, not hurt." The gang asks the same way: they want them
  paying, not dead.
- Hurting the one who owes ends the favour, and the gang texts you why. It used to run on and end with "Somebody else
  handled it", which read as if somebody else had done the killing. When time runs out the gang now says they paid up,
  or, for a lookout, that somebody else ran them off. Somebody you have already hurt is never the one picked.
- A lookout favour's marker says they will fight: put them down or make them run.

**The Hub and the map**
- **A gathering's map pin shows the hour it starts.** It said "near here now" from the moment the street heard of it,
  often an hour early. Word of a funeral, an initiation, an execution or a hunt said "tonight" at any hour; it says
  "later".
- **A Rival's file opens with whole sentences that match their card.** The one coming for you read "at large" under a
  card that said HUNTING YOU, and a dead Rival read "quiet". One who owes you their life says so, on the card too
  ("owes you" alone reads as money).
- **The Crew page names the four ways to get people, as they work.** It said only a gang that "would die for you"
  lends you one; a Friendly gang does too. "Ask them where they lie" is now "while they are on the ground, tell them to
  work for you". The cut says "due within a day" instead of "tomorrow" when it is hours away.
- Informants are listed as "Your informants" (it said "Who owes you"), and a crew member who owes you a life says
  "owes you a life".
- A bounty hunter's bar says BOUNTY HUNTER with their name, as the scanner does. It said HUNTING YOU over the game's
  name for a stranger.
- The scanner calls your own gang's people YOUR GANG. ONE OF YOURS read like your crew.
- History says what happened: you were asked to put down five in a week, a corporation offered money to kill a gang's
  leader, you killed both would-be leaders.

**Messages and choices**
- **Each one says what you have to do.** A power struggle and a corporation's contract say "kill", because sparing
  does not count. Your gang's duty says five put down "dead or out cold", because both count. An informant in trouble
  says "within the hour, and I'll run your way" instead of "tonight". Open season lasts "the next twenty-four" hours.
- "Rewards for kills" replaces "Bounties" on titles and in Mod Settings. It means what you are paid for killing a
  Rival, a crew or an apex squad, not the price on your head.
- Choices say what they do: "[Quit the gang]", "[Side with them]", and "[Not yet] Stay down. I haven't decided." for a
  downed Rival you can come back to.
- A wannabe you killed is no longer said to have walked away. A bagman is said to have one lookout only when one came.
- Gang names take the right possessive: "the Valentinos' bagman", "Maelstrom's open season".

**Mod Settings**
- Choices read as words: "Hunts only", "Named only", "Turf war", "Quiet life".
- Descriptions name the setting they mean and where it is, for example **Hunt line on the HUD** under **General**.
  Scouts and Lookouts list the real ways to stop them: put them down, pay or scare them into silence (with **Pay the
  street** on), or stay out of a scout's sight.

**Fixed**
- **A scout you get away from no longer sends the crew anyway.** Leave the scout far enough behind that the game lets
  them go, and they have lost you: the crew never comes and the gang cools off, as Mod Settings always said. Before,
  the crew set off about half a minute later.

Your progress carries over.

## 2.5.4 (19 November 2026)

**The map**
- **Only the people after you are marked "Hunting you".** Every marker Cyberbane put on anybody said it: a bagman, a
  gang's people at a sit-down or a funeral, a wannabe, a favour's lookout, a Rival waiting at their place, and the
  passer-by a friendly gang asks you to lean on, who has no gang and so read "Crew - Hunting you". Each now says what
  they are and what to do about them: "Owes the Valentinos" with "A favour: remind them what they owe", a bagman
  "Carrying the take", a Rival "At their place, not looking for you. Yet." A Rival at their place is marked as hunting you once the fight starts.
- A bounty hunter's marker carries their name instead of "Crew", and anybody else after you with no gang is a "Hunter".

Your progress carries over.

## 2.5.3 (19 November 2026)

**Talking to people**
- **Pressing Talk brings up the lines, however close you stand.** Somebody frightened, or leaning on a wall, could get
  their own "Talk" back while you stood next to them. Pressing F then played their stock line and froze them, and the
  game kept Cyberbane's lines hidden until you stepped back out of reach. Their own "Talk" now stays off for the whole
  conversation, and Cyberbane's "Talk" never sits on top of another prompt, so F only ever does one thing.

**The Hub**
- **A new Rival shows on the Rivals page's cards.** A Rival from a gang you had never killed anyone of or paid off showed
  in the list but not in the cards, which said "No Rivals yet". The gang is now on your books as soon as one of its
  people has your name, and the Rival takes their place at the top of it. Existing saves are put right on the next load.
- **No countdown to people nobody could send.** On a new game the Standing page showed a Rival, a gang crew and an apex
  squad each coming at a set time, with nobody against you. It was the time the day's next window opened, not anybody
  on their way. Until somebody could actually come it now says so: "nobody has a grudge against you yet", "no gang is
  after you", "no gang is angry enough to send its best".

Your progress carries over.

## 2.5.2 (19 November 2026)

**Talking to people**
- **"Talk" no longer needs a scan.** Look at somebody standing with others and their own "Talk" prompt stepped aside,
  but the game then put up the next person's in its place, and Cyberbane's waited behind it. Scanning hides every
  prompt, which is why scanning was the one sure way to get Cyberbane's. Now everybody close in front of you steps
  aside, not only the one you look at, and gets their own "Talk" back once you move on.
- Somebody whose own "Talk" came back by itself, after they flinched or turned to look at you, steps aside again.
  It used to hold Cyberbane's off for as long as you stood there.
- Somebody Cyberbane wrongly read as having no "Talk" of their own was turned away on the spot. They get the same
  moment as everybody else now.
- When a thing beside them has a prompt on F (a door, a seat, an item, a spot another mod adds), talk still waits for

## 2.0.0 (17 November 2026)

**Cyberbane 2.0 in one paragraph.** Since 1.9.5, Night City talks back. Walk up to anybody and talk: a real

  be talked to. The more a gang fears you, the more of its people hold back. A gang's file on the Hub
  tells you roughly how many of its people will.

  screen, the smaller the crew, down to about three in five of a full one.
