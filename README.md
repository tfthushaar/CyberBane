# Cyberbane

A reputation overhaul for Cyberpunk 2077. Night City notices what V does and acts on it: crowds get
out of your way, gangs decide whether to respect you or hunt you, and the people you hurt come back
with names and grudges.

**Download:** [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/35847)

**Version 2.7.2.** Works on Cyberpunk 2077 **2.5** and **2.31** with the same download.
What changed in each version: [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## What it does

- **You build a reputation.** Fights, jobs, chrome and the way you dress all count. Word spreads
  district by district, so you can be a legend in Watson while Pacifica has never heard of you.
- **The street reacts.** People glance, step aside, freeze or run, depending on how frightening you
  are. Cover your chrome or wear a mask and they can't place you.
- **Gangs pick a side.** Help a gang's enemies and they warm to you: better prices on their turf and
  warnings when their enemies come for you. Keep hurting them and they send a crew, then their apex
  squad, by car or on foot. You can pay them off.
- **Rivals.** Enemies who escape you, or whom you beat and leave alive, come back later with a name,
  a rank, scars from how you hurt them, and their own people. Spare them, kill them, or let them climb.
- **Turf wars.** A gang you keep hurting loses its streets to the gang at war with it. Small outfits like the Mox
  guard their own blocks and only take over a district once everyone else there has been bled dry.
- **A name you earn.** Before the Heist you're a merc few people have heard of. Gangs and Rivals take longer to come
  for you, and no gang sends its apex squad until your Street Cred reaches 15. It grows into the full thing by Street
  Cred 20.
- **Messages in your phone.** Gangs, Rivals and street contacts text you, and some threads let you
  answer: pay a gang off, or haggle over where a Rival is hiding.
- **The Hub.** A Cyberbane tab in the game menu shows your standing, every gang, who holds each
  district, your Rivals, your crew, your titles and a history of what you did, as cards you can
  read at a glance, and explains why the city treats you the way it does.

### New in 2.0

- **Talk to anybody.** Walk up to someone with empty hands and press F. Ask questions, buy a Rival's
  hiding place, pay for safe passage, or talk down a ganger who is about to draw.
- **Make the street talk.** Grab a ganger, crouch over one you put down, or scan one, and they give
  something up, sometimes a Rival's fatal flaw.
- **Rivals with a life.** Catchphrases, family who take a fallen Rival's place, call-outs to a place
  they name, bodyguards, and mercy as your choice.
- **A living street.** Bagmen to rob, favours for friendly gangs, and sit-downs between gangs at war
  that you can crash (now under **Gang life** in Mod Settings).

### New in 2.7

- **Much Better Contracts, side by side (2.7.5).** Detected on its own. Its contracts are jobs to Cyberbane, it sends
  nobody into a fight while one is running, and each contract you are paid for counts as a finished job. A car you took
  from a gang is never deleted with you in it.
- **Your crew, as you know them (2.7.4).** Each of your crew keeps the look they turned up in, pick lists scroll past ten,

  foot come in ahead of you while you drive, and ordinary crew members leave their loot on their bodies, not in your
  pockets.
- **Nobody is unkillable (2.7.2).** However tough a Rival, an apex squad or a hunter gets, Cyberbane keeps them within
  reach of your own weapons, counting their armour, and nothing it sends heals while you are hurting it.

  the Tyger Claws at Wakako's, the Voodoo Boys at their chapel and the Animals at the Grand Imperial Mall. The first time
  you are there it goes on your map, and when you come by and none of them are about, a few are there to talk to.
  Anybody of theirs will tell you where it is.
- **The crew at home (2.7.2).** Put up to three of your crew on **Watch the house** and they stand at your building's
  door and car park while you are home. Near your stash, you can give one of them a gun from it, and it stays in the
  stash with all its mods. Talk to any of your crew to ask how they are holding up, what their old gang was like, and
  whether they know anything about a Rival from it.
- **Crew jobs and the kitty (2.7.2).** Send one of your crew on a hit on one of your Rivals or after a gang's bag, with
  their chance shown before you pick. What the crew brings in goes into a kitty that pays their cut; take it when you
  are home, or have one of them run it to your place. The Tyger Claws and the Valentinos sometimes challenge your crew,
  one of theirs against one of yours, and a robbed bag can hold goods to sell to another gang.
- **Rivals worth their names (2.7.2).** A Rival who climbs comes back as one of their gang's signature fighters: mantis
  blades, gorilla arms, a heavy machine gun, a netrunner's deck. A top Rival may take over a place you cleared on a gig,
  with lieutenants and a squad, and wait there for you; kill them and the gang loses it, and what they kept there is on
  the floor to go through.
- **Meetings you can watch (2.7.2).** Turn up early to a sit-down or a deal and watch: they walk in from further out
  now, and if they spot you watching, the street tells you.
- **Home ground (2.7.1).** The building around each of your apartments counts as home: the lobby, the corridors and the
  car park. Nobody Cyberbane sends comes for you there, people already looking for you don't find you there, and no
  bagmen, gang events or street fights start there. A fight that followed you in carries on. Cyberbane learns each home
  the first time you are inside it or sleep there. **Home ground** under **How often they come** sets it, and can let a
  gang at Vendetta through.
- **A moment of peace (2.7.1).** After any fight, everybody rests: no Rival, crew or apex squad is lined up until the
  breather is over, and **Minutes between encounters** now goes up to 120. A night in your own bed buys a few quiet
  hours as well.
- **Lose them before they get there (2.7.1).** Once a gang has sent people, changing how you look, putting a mask on,
  leaving the district, riding the metro or going home breaks the trail. One of those and they come in looking in the
  wrong place; two or more and they may lose you before they set off. A gang at Vendetta, one with a tracker on you,
  one you have just hurt and one whose streets you are standing on still know where you are.
- **What your mask does (2.7.1).** The Standing page says what a mask or a gang's colours are doing for you. It works
  with or without They Will Remember.
- **Ready to translate (2.7.1).** Every line Cyberbane shows is in one file, and a translation installs as a download of
  its own. See [Translating](#translating).

**The street**
- **The street reads you.** Witnesses run to tell the gang unless you stop them. Night, rain and your own

**The gangs**
- **A code for each gang.** Every gang respects, forgives and fights in its own way, talks like itself, and
  adapts to how you fight it. The Hub tells you each code as you learn it.
- **Gang life.** Funerals, celebrations, initiations, recruiting drives, arms deals and rackets happen at a set time
  somewhere in your district, by one of the data terminals you've found, and the place is pinned on your map. Get
  there, then crash them, pay your respects, or let the gang grow stronger.
- **Gangs fight gangs.** Executions, street fights and hunts you can step into, power struggles where you decide
  who runs a gang, and rival gangers clashing on contested streets.
- **The turf war has money in it.** Gangs have war chests and manpower; back one for a weekly cut, set two on
  each other with a lie, watch rivals unite against you, and at the very top, take streets of your own.

**Being hunted**
- **Crews with a sergeant**, mixed from the people you've met, and apex squads that hunt you as a team. Talk a
  sergeant down before the shooting starts.
- **Fairer hunts.** A moment's grace when they find you, trails that go cold when you lie low, scouts you can
  stop or pay off before they call you in, and lookouts in the crowd.
- **The hunt grows.** Trackers planted by quickhacks, open season from a gang at the top of its anger, crews lying
  in wait on your road, and the Tyger Claws' assassin.
- **A price on your head.** Gangs, Rivals and the corporations put bounties on you, and named solos from the
  hunters' ladder come to collect. Beat them to climb the ladder, pay the price off from the Hub, or kill whoever put
  it up. The corporations hire killers now instead of sending crews.

**Rivals**
- **Rivals with a story.** A facedown before the shooting starts, humiliation, Rivals back from the dead, Rivals at
  war with each other and bound to each other, trophies, chrome that answers how you hurt them, and last words.
- **Their places.** A Rival keeps hours at a place of their own. One of their people who spots you runs to warn
  them, and if the runner gets away they move a few streets over. Word that a Rival is getting new chrome can tell you
  which ripperdoc they are laid up at.

**Your people**
- **A crew of your own.** Rivals who owe you, gangers you frightened, solos you pay and people a friendly gang lends
  you work for you. Call up to three when you are hunted, and keep them loyal, or one day they sell you out or turn
  on you and come back as a Rival.
- **Friends on the street.** Borrow a friendly gang's people to watch your back, become one of a gang, ransom the
  ones who give up, and feed the street lies.

**The Hub and the map**
- **The Hub in cards.** Every gang, district, Rival and crew member as a card in the game's own style, the gangs after
  you first. Click a gang and every other card shows how it stands with that one.
- **The map remembers.** Pins for a day where a Rival fell, a gathering was crashed, a district changed hands or one
  of your crew died, and, with TweakXL, a CYBERBANE box in the map's filters for all of Cyberbane's pins.

## Install

**Vortex:** add the zip with "Install From File", enable it and Deploy.

**By hand:** extract the zip into your Cyberpunk 2077 folder (the one with `bin` and `r6`). The
scripts end up in `r6\scripts\Cyberbane\`. To update, extract the new zip over the old one.

To uninstall, delete `r6\scripts\Cyberbane` or disable the mod in Vortex. Your Cyberbane progress
lives in your saves: a save made while the mod is removed loses it.

## How to play

- **The Hub:** open the game menu (Inventory, Map, Character...) and pick the **Cyberbane** tab, after
  Journal. With the game's radial menu, open Character and it is beside it. Can't find it? Turn on
  **Hub in the pause menu** (Mod Settings > Cyberbane > Messages and HUD) and it is under Escape instead.
  Click a gang to pay them off, click a Rival to read their file, buy where they are hiding or forget
  them, and call or pay your crew. Pick one of your crew and press **Orders** to kit them out, train or
  spar with them, or send them to work or to war; **Name, colours, the fallen** is for the crew itself.
  **Show as a list** on a page turns its cards into a list.
- **Talking:** look at somebody up close with empty hands, press F (A on a controller), scroll to pick
  a line. Reach for a weapon and the conversation ends. With a weapon in your hand, the prompt shows
  **Holster to talk** instead, and a greyed prompt also says when somebody just spoke to you, is busy
  fighting or is part of a job. Vendors, joytoys, fixers and ripperdocs keep the game's own conversations.
  While you talk to one of a gang, their people nearby hold their fire, and on a gang's restricted ground the warning to
  leave waits until you are done. Afterwards they are as they were, unless the talk changed that. Hurt one of them and
  the hold is off. On hostile ground, where they shoot on sight, step out first.
- **Calling your crew:** open the **My crew** thread on your phone and pick **Need you. Now.**, press it on the Hub's
  Crew page, or set a key for it (Mod Settings > Cyberbane > Your crew > **Call your crew (key)**). In a fight, up to
  three come and go home once it is over; with nothing to fight, two come and walk with you until you send them home
  (**Go home. I've got this.** on the phone, **Send them home** on the Hub, or the same key). They come to gigs and side
  jobs; **Your crew on jobs** changes that. Cutscenes, the main story's own missions and safe places like your
  apartment keep them out. If they can't come, or have to leave, they say why. They can be killed like anybody else, and the fallen go
  on the memorial; turn off **Your crew can die** and the game protects them the way it protects its own companions.
- **Your crew at home:** on a member's **Orders**, the Work row goes through earning, recruiting and **Watch the
  house**. Members on watch stand at your building's door and car park while you are on home ground: once you have gone
  in and out of the apartment and parked there, they know where. Stand near your stash and **Give them a weapon** lists
  its guns too. Talk to one of your crew (look at them, press F) for **How are you holding up?**, **Tell me about** their
  old gang and **Heard anything?**
- **Jobs, the kitty and challenges:** on a member's **Orders**, **Send them on a job** lists the Rivals they can go
  after (from Veteran) and the gangs whose bag they can take (from Soldier), each with their chance. The kitty, and a
  challenge waiting for an answer, are on the Crew page under **Name, colours, the fallen**; the kitty is taken when you
  are on home ground. **Running the money** is a kind of work on the Work row. Goods from a robbed bag are sold by
  talking to somebody of another gang.
- **Finding a gang:** ask one of theirs **Where do your people hang out?**, or just go. A gang's home goes on the map
  the first time you are there, hidden while that gang is hostile to you.
- **Recruiting a Rival:** beat a Rival without killing them, look at them where they lie and press F, and spare them.
  The next time you put that Rival down, the same talk offers **[Recruit] You owe me. Work for me.** A Rival you have
  spared twice may also text you, and you can ask them in the thread. Crouching over somebody down is for questioning
  their people; a Rival on the ground is talked to.
- Cyberbane sets no keys of its own. The only one it offers, for calling your crew, is yours to choose.

## Settings

Everything is in **Mod Settings > Cyberbane**. The first page, **General**, has what most people
change: Cyberbane on or off, a preset (Subtle, Default, Dramatic, or one with a shape: Nemesis, Turf war,
Quiet life), where messages go, the hunt line
at the top of the screen, and map markers. The pages after it go deeper, one subject each, with
**Advanced** at the bottom.

- **Fewer interruptions:** set **Where messages go** to Off. Nothing about how the mod plays changes.
- **Words gone before you can read them:** raise **How long words stay on screen**, first on the
  **Messages and HUD** page. It covers messages, phone pop-ups, what you overhear and every line of a
  conversation.
- **Set how the gangs see you:** **How gangs see you** puts every gang, or any one of them, at Allied,
  Friendly, Neutral, Hostile or Vendetta, whatever you have done. **Earned in play** puts it back.
- **Leave a gang alone:** switch it off under **Factions**. Cyberbane stops reacting for that gang
  and keeps your standing for later. The NCPD can be switched off the same way.
- **How often you are hunted:** each gang comes by its own grudge against you, and Rivals by theirs. **How eager gangs
  are** and **How eager Rivals are** under **How often they come** stretch or shorten every wait, **Apex squads** turns
  those off, and **Minutes between encounters** is the breather after each fight, up to two hours of play. With
  **Everybody rests after a fight** on, that breather holds back Rivals, crews and apex squads alike. **Quiet after a
  night at home** keeps them away for a few game hours after you sleep in your own bed, and **Home ground** keeps the
  building around your apartments quiet. **Before you're known**, on the same page, keeps the early game gentler until
  your Street Cred reaches 20; switch it off to be known from day one.
- **Hiding from a crew:** a mask makes every gang slower to find you, and their colours make their people slower to pick
  you out. With **Lie low** on (under **Being hunted**), changing how you look, masking up, leaving the district, riding
  the metro or going home after a gang has sent people can make them lose you before they arrive.
- **Gang homes and guards:** **Gang homes** under **Gang life** switches the gangs' places off. **Guards at home**,
  under **Your crew**, sets how many can watch the house (0 to 3, 0 for none).
- **Crew jobs, Rivals' chrome and strongholds:** **Crew jobs and the kitty** and **Challenges to your crew** are under
  **Your crew**, **Goods in stolen bags** under **Gang life**, and **Signature chrome** and **Strongholds** under
  **Rivals**. All are on by default.
- **Fewer messages:** **How much you hear**, on the first page, spaces out the street's chatter (word going round and
  lines overheard). Warnings, demands, offers, your crew and answers to what you did always come through.
- **Start over:** **Reset all Cyberbane data** under **Advanced** (or Start over on the Hub's History
  page) wipes this playthrough's reputation, and older saves of it are wiped too when they load, so
  loading one does not bring it back. Changed your mind? **Undo the last Start over** on the same page,
  then load a save from before it: its reputation, Rivals, crew and titles are back. Where your Rivals
  were last seen and how hard you had hit each gang are gone either way.

Each playthrough keeps its own record, and nothing one character does reaches another.

## For mod authors

Cyberbane has an extension API (2.7.4), so an add-on can use it without calling Cyberbane's internals, which change
between releases. Everything is on the `CyberbaneAPI` class; guard each call with `@if(ModuleExists("Cyberbane.Api"))`.
A later level adds its own marker module (`Cyberbane.Api2`, ...), so an add-on built for a newer level compiles to nothing
on an older Cyberbane instead of breaking every script mod on the install.

- **API level 1 (`Cyberbane.Api`, 2.7.4):**
  - Listen: extend `CBListener` (kills, Rival events, posture changes, the world settling, Start over with its epoch, a new
    playthrough) and `RegisterListener` each session. Calls arrive on Cyberbane's tick, never inside a game event.
  - Read: `ApiLevel`, `IsEnabled`, `MessagesMode`, `HowMuchYouHear`, `FactionOn`, `Preset`, `BeforeYoureKnownHolds`,
    `GangCodesOn`, `PlaythroughEpoch`, and the older `GetRenown`, `GetDread`, `GetStanding`.
  - Ask: `RequestGroup` (people, through Cyberbane's gates and its 18-spawn ceiling), `RequestConvoy` (a convoy that travels
    a route), `PinPlace`, `RegisterSender` + `SendAside` and `AskPriced` (texts and priced questions), `AdjustStanding` with
    a reason, `RegisterEventKind` + `StartEvent` (Cyberbane's one event slot, its clock and holds), `RegisterGangsRow` and
    `RegisterPage` (cards on the Hub, as data), `MakeFactionProfile` + `RegisterFaction`.
- **API level 2 (`Cyberbane.Api2`, 2.7.5):**
  - Clocks and place: `CBListener.OnTick` (every couple of seconds on the settled tick), `WorldStable`, `ClockWord`,
    `CurrentDistrict`, `PlaceName`, `FindPlaces` (fast-travel points of a district between two distances from V).
  - Sides: `SetGroupsHostile`, `SetGroupsNeutral`, `SetGroupConvoyHostile`, `SetGroupConvoyNeutral`, body to body, kept as
    people arrive and taken back when either side is released. Groups asked for on V's side fight V's enemies.
  - Things in the world: `RequestVehicle` (empty, out of V's sight, optionally wrecked) + `VehicleWhyNot` +
    `ReleaseVehicle`; `DropLoot` (the game's drop bag near V, tier 1 to 5); `MovePin`.
  - Convoys: `ConvoyOutcome` (arrived, broken, got away, released, unknown), `ConvoyAlive`, `ConvoyBodies`;
    `ConvoyState` answers -1 for a handle it does not know.
  - Names and records: `FactionName`, `FactionLabel` (a registered faction's `nameKey` and `labelKey`), `RivalIds`,
    `RivalFaction`, `RivalRank`, `RivalName`, `RivalAlive`, `FileHistory` (a History line that moves no standing; keys
    carry a dash), `StartEventFrom` (an event announced by your own contact).
- An API function is never removed or renamed; a retired one stays and does nothing. Every change is compiled on the
  game patches Cyberbane supports.
- Keep your own saved data in your own `ScriptableSystem`, your own localization prefix, and your own settings page.

## Compatibility

- **They Will Remember / Night City Remembers:** detected automatically, nothing to set. Cyberbane lets it decide
  who is hostile to you, and the Gangs page marks the gangs it decides with "(TWR)" or "(NCR)". Somebody whose gang
  it has hostile won't talk to you; the prompt says so. They Will Remember also takes over crews and disguises.
  Night City Remembers sends nobody after you, so Cyberbane's crews still come. Set
  **They Will Remember / Night City Remembers** to Off under **Advanced** to let Cyberbane decide instead.
- **Much Better AI, Street Sense, New Game Plus:** detected automatically; Cyberbane steps back
  where they already cover something.
- **Much Better Contracts (Repeatable Gigs):** detected automatically. Its contracts are jobs to Cyberbane: nobody
  Cyberbane sends comes for you at a contract's site, and while a contract is running Cyberbane sends nobody new into a
  fight. Each contract it pays you for counts as a finished job for your Renown; its pay is its own, with no gig bonus on
  top.
- **Smasher Encounters:** detected automatically. While Smasher is after you, Cyberbane sends nobody new and turns nobody
  on you at sight, and his Arasaka backup doesn't break and run. Your crew still comes when called.
- **Equipment-EX and other outfit mods:** your outfit is read as it looks on V.
- **Phone Extension:** works alongside.
- **Companions** (the game's and modded ones) fight the people Cyberbane sends after you.
- **Story characters, quest NPCs, AMM and romance mods** are never touched.
- **Safe areas:** nothing happens in apartments, clinics, bars and other places the game treats as
  safe. Two switches under **Safe areas and jobs** change that. The building around your apartments is home ground
  (see Settings).

## Translating

Every line Cyberbane shows is in `r6\scripts\Cyberbane\Localization\en-us\English.reds`. To translate it:

1. Copy that file into a folder of your own beside Cyberbane's, for example `r6\scripts\Cyberbane_de\German.reds`, so
   updating Cyberbane never overwrites your work.
2. In your copy, rename `CBLocalizationEnUs` to `CyberbaneText_` and your game language with its dash as an underscore:
   `CyberbaneText_de_de`, `CyberbaneText_fr_fr`, `CyberbaneText_pt_br` and so on. Change the `module` line to one of your
   own.
3. Translate the second text in each line and leave the first, the key, as it is. `{0}` and `{1}` are filled in by the
   game and can go wherever your language needs them.

The top of the file explains the rest. Lines you have not translated yet show in English, and the changelog says which
lines each release adds. Share your translation as a mod of its own on Nexus, with Cyberbane as a requirement.

## Reporting a bug

Post on the mod's Nexus page with your game version, your list of mods, and what you were doing when it
happened. A screenshot helps a lot: the Hub's Standing and Gangs pages say why the city is treating you
the way it is, so a picture of the page that fits your problem often explains it.

- **A red error at start-up** means a script failed to load and Cyberbane is not running. Send
  `r6\logs\redscript_rCURRENT.log` from your game folder.
- **A crash to desktop:** if you have a crash reporter installed (for example Advanced Crash Reporter),
  send its newest report.
- **Anything else, with Cyber Engine Tweaks installed:** send `bin\x64\plugins\cyber_engine_tweaks\gamelog.log`.
  Cyberbane writes what it was doing there, including why somebody would not talk to you.
