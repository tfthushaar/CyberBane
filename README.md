# Cyberbane

A reputation overhaul for Cyberpunk 2077. Night City notices what V does and acts on it: crowds get
out of your way, gangs decide whether to respect you or hunt you, and the people you hurt come back
with names and grudges.

**Download:** [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/35847)

**Version 2.6.5.** Works on Cyberpunk 2077 **2.5** and **2.31** with the same download.
What changed in each version: [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## What it does

- **You build a reputation.** Fights, jobs, chrome and the way you dress all count. Word spreads
  district by district, so you can be a legend in Watson while Pacifica has never heard of you.
- **The street reacts.** People glance, step aside, freeze or run, depending on how frightening you
  are. Cover your chrome or wear a mask and they can't place you.
- **Gangs pick a side.** Help a gang's enemies and they warm to you: better prices on their turf and
  warnings when their enemies come for you. Keep hurting them and they send a crew, then their apex
  squad, by car or on foot. You can pay them off.
- **Rivals.** Enemies who escape you, or whom you beat and leave alive, come back later with a name,
  a rank, scars from how you hurt them, and their own people. Spare them, kill them, or let them climb.
- **Turf wars.** A gang you keep hurting loses its streets to the gang at war with it.
- **Messages in your phone.** Gangs, Rivals and street contacts text you, and some threads let you
  answer: pay a gang off, or haggle over where a Rival is hiding.
- **The Hub.** A Cyberbane tab in the game menu shows your standing, every gang, who holds each
  district, your Rivals, your crew, your titles and a history of what you did, as cards you can
  read at a glance, and explains why the city treats you the way it does.

### New in 2.0

- **Talk to anybody.** Walk up to someone with empty hands and press F. Ask questions, buy a Rival's
  hiding place, pay for safe passage, or talk down a ganger who is about to draw.
- **Make the street talk.** Grab a ganger, crouch over one you put down, or scan one, and they give
  something up, sometimes a Rival's fatal flaw.
- **Rivals with a life.** Catchphrases, family who take a fallen Rival's place, call-outs to a place
  they name, bodyguards, and mercy as your choice.
- **A living street.** Bagmen to rob, favours for friendly gangs, and sit-downs between gangs at war
  that you can crash (now under **Gang life** in Mod Settings).

### New in 2.6

- **Grudges, not a timetable (2.6.5).** Each gang comes for you by how it stands with you: a fresh wound brings them sooner,
  a gang you have broken barely comes at all, and distance, their own wars, your mask and the hour all count. The Hub's
  Standing page says whose grudge runs out next.
- **A street with stories (2.6.5).** A bagman walking the take to his gang's drop, debts that lead to more debts, somebody
  who saw your Rival met on the way there, wannabes with friends watching, ransoms paid at a drop (or not), and gang
  sit-downs held over something.
- **Dogtown's Scrappers (2.6.4).** The scavengers of Dogtown's ruins are a gang of their own, with a code, a voice, names
  and a card on the Hub. They share Dogtown with Barghest. Needs Phantom Liberty.
- **A crew that comes when you call (2.6.3).** Call them from the phone, the Hub or a key of your own, to gigs, side
  jobs and gang turf, and with nothing to fight they walk with you until you send them home. When they can't come,
  they say exactly why.
- **Words you can read (2.6.3).** Conversations and messages stay up as long as they take to read, one after another,
  and **How long words stay on screen** stretches them all. A weapon in your hand shows **Holster to talk** where Talk
  would be.
- **Enemies measured against you.** Apex squads, Rivals and bounty hunters read your level, attributes, armour, best
  weapon and chrome from the game, and come ready for how you fight.
- **A crew that can outgrow an apex squad**, with its rank and the way to the next one on every card, and Cyberbane's
  people by name in your scanner.
- **Pick a side** in a street fight between two gangs, find a gang's people at their **hangouts**, and news that takes
  time to get round.
- **Your crew, kitted out.** Hand them a weapon from your bag, have spare chrome put in, send them to train, spar with
  them, put them to work earning or recruiting, and send a senior one to start a war on a gang. All from the Hub's Crew
  page. Details in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- **Your crew as a crew.** Give it a name and colours. It has its own standing with every gang, a card of its own on
  the Gangs page, and a memorial for the ones who died, with what they carried.

### New in 2.5

Everything added since 2.3.0, in one place. The fixes in each update are in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

**The street**
- **The street reads you.** Witnesses run to tell the gang unless you stop them. Night, rain and your own
  blood change who notices you. Each district has its own temper, and places remember what you did there.
- **Known by your car and gun.** Use the same ones and a mask stops hiding you. A gang's bosses can see
  through a disguise.
- **Wannabes** square up to you for a name, fists only, and the street hears how it went.
- **Pay the street** to keep watch for you, or to forget what they saw.
- **The street tells it.** Lines that know who you are and what you did, the morning brief, the day's headlines and
  your legend on the Hub, voices, panic in a crew you pick off unseen, and answering gangs and Rivals by text.

**The gangs**
- **A code for each gang.** Every gang respects, forgives and fights in its own way, talks like itself, and
  adapts to how you fight it. The Hub tells you each code as you learn it.
- **Gang life.** Funerals, celebrations, initiations, recruiting drives, arms deals and rackets happen near you
  at a set time. Crash them, pay your respects, or let the gang grow stronger.
- **Gangs fight gangs.** Executions, street fights and hunts you can step into, power struggles where you decide
  who runs a gang, and rival gangers clashing on contested streets.
- **The turf war has money in it.** Gangs have war chests and manpower; back one for a weekly cut, set two on
  each other with a lie, watch rivals unite against you, and at the very top, take streets of your own.
- **Peace with terms**: haggle a gang's price by text, or trade a favour for it.

**Being hunted**
- **Crews with a sergeant**, mixed from the people you've met, and apex squads that hunt you as a team. Talk a
  sergeant down before the shooting starts.
- **Fairer hunts.** A moment's grace when they find you, trails that go cold when you lie low, scouts you can
  stop or pay off before they call you in, and lookouts in the crowd.
- **The hunt grows.** Trackers planted by quickhacks, open season from a gang at the top of its anger, crews lying
  in wait on your road, and the Tyger Claws' assassin.
- **A price on your head.** Gangs, Rivals and the corporations put bounties on you, and named solos from the
  hunters' ladder come to collect. Beat them to climb the ladder, pay the price off from the Hub, or kill whoever put
  it up. The corporations hire killers now instead of sending crews.

**Rivals**
- **Rivals with a story.** A facedown before the shooting starts, humiliation, Rivals back from the dead, Rivals at
  war with each other and bound to each other, trophies, chrome that answers how you hurt them, and last words.
- **Their places.** A Rival keeps hours at a place of their own. One of their people who spots you runs to warn
  them, and if the runner gets away they move a few streets over. Word that a Rival is getting new chrome can tell you
  which ripperdoc they are laid up at.

**Your people**
- **A crew of your own.** Rivals who owe you, gangers you frightened, solos you pay and people a friendly gang lends
  you work for you. Call up to three when you are hunted, and keep them loyal, or one day they sell you out or turn
  on you and come back as a Rival.
- **Friends on the street.** Borrow a friendly gang's people to watch your back, become one of a gang, ransom the
  ones who give up, and feed the street lies.

**The Hub and the map**
- **The Hub in cards.** Every gang, district, Rival and crew member as a card in the game's own style, the gangs after
  you first. Click a gang and every other card shows how it stands with that one.
- **The map remembers.** Pins for a day where a Rival fell, a gathering was crashed, a district changed hands or one
  of your crew died, and, with TweakXL, a CYBERBANE box in the map's filters for all of Cyberbane's pins.

## Install

**Vortex:** add the zip with "Install From File", enable it and Deploy.

**By hand:** extract the zip into your Cyberpunk 2077 folder (the one with `bin` and `r6`). The
scripts end up in `r6\scripts\Cyberbane\`. To update, extract the new zip over the old one.

To uninstall, delete `r6\scripts\Cyberbane` or disable the mod in Vortex. Your Cyberbane progress
lives in your saves: a save made while the mod is removed loses it.

## How to play

- **The Hub:** open the game menu (Inventory, Map, Character...) and pick the **Cyberbane** tab, after
  Journal. With the game's radial menu, open Character and it is beside it. Can't find it? Turn on
  **Hub in the pause menu** (Mod Settings > Cyberbane > Messages and HUD) and it is under Escape instead.
  Click a gang to pay them off, click a Rival to read their file, buy where they are hiding or forget
  them, and call or pay your crew. Pick one of your crew and press **Orders** to kit them out, train or
  spar with them, or send them to work or to war; **Name, colours, the fallen** is for the crew itself.
  **Show as a list** on a page turns its cards into a list.
- **Talking:** look at somebody up close with empty hands, press F (A on a controller), scroll to pick
  a line. Reach for a weapon and the conversation ends. With a weapon in your hand, the prompt shows
  **Holster to talk** instead, and a greyed prompt also says when somebody just spoke to you, is busy
  fighting or is part of a job. Vendors, joytoys, fixers and ripperdocs keep the game's own conversations.
- **Calling your crew:** open the **My crew** thread on your phone and pick **Need you. Now.**, press it on the Hub's
  Crew page, or set a key for it (Mod Settings > Cyberbane > Your crew > **Call your crew (key)**). In a fight, up to
  three come and go home once it is over; with nothing to fight, two come and walk with you until you send them home
  (**Go home. I've got this.** on the phone, **Send them home** on the Hub, or the same key). They come to gigs and side
  jobs; **Your crew on jobs** changes that. Cutscenes, the main story's own missions and safe places like your
  apartment keep them out. If they can't come, or have to leave, they say why. They can be killed like anybody else, and the fallen go
  on the memorial; turn off **Your crew can die** and the game protects them the way it protects its own companions.
- **Recruiting a Rival:** beat a Rival without killing them, look at them where they lie and press F, and spare them.
  The next time you put that Rival down, the same talk offers **[Recruit] You owe me. Work for me.** A Rival you have
  spared twice may also text you, and you can ask them in the thread. Crouching over somebody down is for questioning
  their people; a Rival on the ground is talked to.
- Cyberbane sets no keys of its own. The only one it offers, for calling your crew, is yours to choose.

## Settings

Everything is in **Mod Settings > Cyberbane**. The first page, **General**, has what most people
change: Cyberbane on or off, a preset (Subtle, Default, Dramatic, or one with a shape: Nemesis, Turf war,
Quiet life), where messages go, the hunt line
at the top of the screen, and map markers. The pages after it go deeper, one subject each, with
**Advanced** at the bottom.

- **Fewer interruptions:** set **Where messages go** to Off. Nothing about how the mod plays changes.
- **Words gone before you can read them:** raise **How long words stay on screen**, first on the
  **Messages and HUD** page. It covers messages, phone pop-ups, what you overhear and every line of a
  conversation.
- **Set how the gangs see you:** **How gangs see you** puts every gang, or any one of them, at Allied,
  Friendly, Neutral, Hostile or Vendetta, whatever you have done. **Earned in play** puts it back.
- **Leave a gang alone:** switch it off under **Factions**. Cyberbane stops reacting for that gang
  and keeps your standing for later. The NCPD can be switched off the same way.
- **How often you are hunted:** each gang comes by its own grudge against you, and Rivals by theirs. **How eager gangs
  are** and **How eager Rivals are** under **How often they come** stretch or shorten every wait, **Apex squads** turns
  those off, and **Encounter gap** is the breather after each fight.
- **Fewer messages:** **How much you hear**, on the first page, spaces out the street's chatter (word going round and
  lines overheard). Warnings, demands, offers, your crew and answers to what you did always come through.
- **Start over:** **Reset all Cyberbane data** under **Advanced** (or Start over on the Hub's History
  page) wipes this playthrough's reputation, and older saves of it are wiped too when they load, so
  loading one does not bring it back. Changed your mind? **Undo the last Start over** on the same page,
  then load a save from before it: its reputation, Rivals, crew and titles are back. Where your Rivals
  were last seen and how hard you had hit each gang are gone either way.

Each playthrough keeps its own record, and nothing one character does reaches another.

## Compatibility

- **They Will Remember / Night City Remembers:** detected automatically. Cyberbane lets it decide
  who is hostile to you and how well a disguise works, and follows both: the Gangs page marks the
  gangs it decides with "(TWR)". Set **They Will Remember compatibility** to Off under **Advanced** to let Cyberbane decide instead.
- **Much Better AI, Street Sense, New Game Plus:** detected automatically; Cyberbane steps back
  where they already cover something.
- **Equipment-EX and other outfit mods:** your outfit is read as it looks on V.
- **Phone Extension:** works alongside.
- **Companions** (the game's and modded ones) fight the people Cyberbane sends after you.
- **Story characters, quest NPCs, AMM and romance mods** are never touched.
- **Safe areas:** nothing happens in apartments, clinics, bars and other places the game treats as
  safe. Two switches under **Safe areas and jobs** change that.

## Reporting a bug

Post on the mod's Nexus page with your game version, your list of mods, and what you were doing when it
happened. A screenshot helps a lot: the Hub's Standing and Gangs pages say why the city is treating you
the way it is, so a picture of the page that fits your problem often explains it.

- **A red error at start-up** means a script failed to load and Cyberbane is not running. Send
  `r6\logs\redscript_rCURRENT.log` from your game folder.
- **A crash to desktop:** if you have a crash reporter installed (for example Advanced Crash Reporter),
  send its newest report.
- **Anything else, with Cyber Engine Tweaks installed:** send `bin\x64\plugins\cyber_engine_tweaks\gamelog.log`.
  Cyberbane writes what it was doing there, including why somebody would not talk to you.
